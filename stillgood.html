Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the StillGood? app ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use it.

1. Information We Collect

The App does not require an account and does not collect or transmit personal information to our own servers. All data you create in the App - your food items, expiry dates, categories, notes, and settings - is stored locally on your device only.

The App uses the third-party services described below (Google AdMob, Unity Ads, Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, and public food product databases), which collect limited device and usage information directly.

2. Camera and Product Lookup

If you choose to scan a barcode or read an expiry date with your camera, the camera image is processed on your device using Google ML Kit. The App does not save, upload, or share these images.

When you scan a barcode, the barcode number may be sent to public food product databases (Open Food Facts, the USDA FoodData Central, and the Korean Food Safety Korea open API) to look up the product name. Only the barcode number is sent; no personal information or images are included. These services may receive technical information such as your IP address as part of the request and handle it under their own policies.

3. Advertising

The App displays ads served by Google AdMob, and may show ads from Unity Ads through AdMob mediation. To serve ads, these services and their partners may collect and use device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), IP address, and other technical information, and may use this information to show personalized or non-personalized ads. This data collection is governed by their own privacy policies, including Google's, available at https://policies.google.com/privacy, and Unity's, available at https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy. You can control ad personalization in your device's settings (e.g., Settings > Privacy > Ads on Android). Where required (for example, in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland), the App asks for your consent before showing personalized ads, and you can change your choice later from the App's Settings.

4. Analytics and Crash Reports

The App uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics, services provided by Google, to understand how the App is used and to receive reports when the App crashes so we can fix problems. This information includes technical details such as the device model, operating system version, app version, the state of the App at the time of a crash, and a randomly generated installation identifier. It does not include your name, email address, or the content of your food list. Firebase's data handling is described at https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

5. Permissions

  • Camera: used only to scan barcodes and read expiry dates, as described in Section 2.
  • Notifications: used to remind you before your items expire. The App asks for permission to show notifications and for permission to schedule exact alarms so reminders arrive on time, and it re-registers reminders after the device restarts.
  • Internet access: used to look up products, load ads, and send the analytics and crash reports described above.
  • Advertising ID: used by the advertising services described in Section 3.

The App does not request access to your contacts, location, microphone, or storage.

6. Reviews

The App may invite you to rate it using Google Play's in-app review feature. Any rating or review you submit is handled by Google Play under Google's privacy policy; the App never sees what you write.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share it with third parties, other than the barcode lookups described in Section 2, the advertising data described in Section 3, and the analytics and crash reports described in Section 4, which are collected directly by the third-party services, not by us.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Since all of your food data is stored locally on your device, you can delete it at any time with Settings > Reset All Data in the App, by clearing the App's data, or by uninstalling the App. See also our Data Deletion page.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
