Privacy Policy

Shopping List  ·  Effective date: 12 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Shopping List app (package co.kr.ezapp.shoppinglist; "we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and shares information about you.

1. Information Collection

We do not require you to create an account or provide personal identification information (such as name, email address, or phone number) to use the app.

  • On-device data: Your shopping lists, items, and settings are stored only locally on your device's database. They are not uploaded to our servers. You can remove them at any time from Settings → Reset All Data, or by uninstalling the app.
  • Device information: Our third-party providers below may collect basic device information (such as device model and OS version) and advertising identifiers.

2. Third-Party Services

The app uses the following third-party services, which may collect information used to identify your device:

  • Google AdMob — displays advertisements, mediating ads from several ad networks — currently including Meta Audience Network — on our behalf. Each network may independently collect advertising identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID) to serve and measure ads.
  • Meta Audience Network — an AdMob mediation partner that may independently collect advertising identifiers and app-usage data to serve and measure ads on our behalf.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase — used to understand feature usage and app performance.
  • Firebase Crashlytics — records crash and stability data so we can diagnose and fix defects.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging — delivers the optional daily reminder notification you enable in-app, using a device-level messaging token rather than any personal identifier.

3. Use of Information

We use the information above to maintain and improve the app's functionality, show relevant advertisements in compliance with Google's and Meta's policies, analyse usage trends, diagnose crashes, and deliver the reminder notifications you opt into. We do not sell your personal information.

4. GDPR (European Users) & CCPA (US Users)

  • GDPR: Users in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland have the right to access, correct, or delete their data. You can manage your consent for personalised ads through the in-app consent dialog, and reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.
  • CCPA: We do not sell your personal information. You have the right to opt out of data collection for advertising purposes via your device's ad settings.

5. Data Retention

Shopping lists and items are stored locally on your device unless otherwise synced with a cloud service you have separately authorised. We do not store your private lists on our servers.

6. Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Material changes will be reflected here with a new effective date.

8. Contact Us

Questions about this Privacy Policy: help.ezapp@gmail.com
