Privacy Policy

Calculator  ·  Effective date: 12 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Calculator app (package com.ezapp.calculator; "we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and shares information about you.

1. Information Collection

We do not require you to create an account or provide personal identification information (such as name, email address, or phone number) to use the app.

  • On-device data: Your calculation history, tape, and all display and behaviour settings (theme, accent colour, decimal places, angle unit, and similar preferences) are stored only locally on your device. They are not transmitted to us. Clearing the app's data, using the in-app "Clear history" option, or uninstalling the app permanently removes them.
  • Device information: Our third-party providers below may collect basic device information (such as device model and OS version) and advertising identifiers.

2. Third-Party Services

The app uses the following third-party services, which may collect information used to identify your device:

  • Google AdMob — displays the advertising banner shown at the bottom of the app, mediating ads from several ad networks — currently including Meta Audience Network — on our behalf. Google may collect advertising identifiers (Android Advertising ID) and general usage data to serve, personalise, measure, and limit fraud in ads. See also How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.
  • Meta Audience Network — an AdMob mediation partner that may independently collect advertising identifiers and app-usage data to serve and measure ads on our behalf.
  • Firebase Analytics — collects anonymous, aggregated feature-usage data (such as which calculator mode is used) tied to an anonymous app-install identifier, not your identity.
  • Firebase Crashlytics — records crash and stability data (crash stack traces, device model, OS version, app state at the time of a crash) so we can diagnose and fix defects.

3. Use of Information

Information is used to keep the app working and stable, to diagnose crashes, to understand which features are used, and to show and measure advertisements in compliance with Google's policies. We do not sell your personal information.

4. GDPR (European Users) & CCPA (US Users)

  • GDPR: Users in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland have the right to access, correct, or delete their data. You can manage your consent for personalised ads through the consent dialog shown by Google's advertising SDK, and you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.
  • CCPA: We do not sell your personal information. You have the right to opt out of data collection for advertising purposes via your device's ad settings.

5. Children's Privacy

The app is a general-audience utility and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Data Retention

Calculation history and settings are stored only on your device, so you control retention — use "Clear history" in Settings, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app to remove everything it stores locally. Data processed by Google (advertising, analytics, crash reporting) is retained and managed according to Google's own policies linked above.

7. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time as the app changes. Material changes will be reflected here with a new effective date.

Questions about this Privacy Policy: help.ezapp@gmail.com
