Data Deletion

Last updated: September 16, 2026

This page explains how to delete data associated with the Tabata Interval Timer app ("the App").

1. Local App Data

The App stores your routines, interval settings, and workout completion history only on your device - the data never leaves your device in the first place. You are always in full control of it:

  • Delete an individual custom routine from within the App's routine list.
  • Clear all App data at once via your device's Settings > Apps > Tabata > Storage > Clear data.
  • Remove everything permanently by uninstalling the App.

2. Advertising ID

Google AdMob serves ads within the App on our behalf and may use your device's advertising ID to do so. You can reset or delete this identifier independently, at any time, from your device's Settings > Privacy > Ads.

3. Requesting Deletion From Us

Because the App does not send your personal data to our own servers, there is normally nothing on our side to delete. If you believe we hold any information about you and would like it removed, contact us with your device model and approximate date of use, and we will look into it and respond within 30 days.

4. Contact Us

For any data deletion questions or requests, please contact us at: help.ezapp@gmail.com
