Data Deletion

Last updated: October 27, 2026

This page explains how to delete data associated with the StillGood? app ("the App").

1. Local App Data

The App stores your food items, expiry dates, categories, notes, and settings only on your device - the data never leaves your device in the first place. You are always in full control of it:

  • Erase all of your items from within the App via Settings > Reset All Data.
  • Clear all App data at once via your device's Settings > Apps > StillGood? > Storage > Clear data.
  • Remove everything permanently by uninstalling the App.

2. Camera Images and Barcode Lookups

Camera images used for scanning barcodes or reading expiry dates are processed on your device and are not saved or uploaded. When you scan a barcode, only the barcode number is sent to public food product databases to look up the product name; no account or personal information is attached, and we hold no copy of it.

3. Advertising ID

Google AdMob and Unity Ads serve ads within the App and may use your device's advertising ID to do so. You can reset or delete this identifier independently, at any time, from your device's Settings > Privacy > Ads.

4. Analytics and Crash Reports

Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics (Google) receive technical usage and crash information. It contains no name, email address, or food list content, and is kept by Firebase for a limited time before being deleted automatically.

5. Requesting Deletion From Us

Because the App does not send your personal data to our own servers, there is normally nothing on our side to delete. If you believe we hold any information about you and would like it removed, contact us with your device model and approximate date of use, and we will look into it and respond within 30 days.

6. Contact Us
