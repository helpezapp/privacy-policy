Data Deletion

Shopping List  ·  Effective date: 12 September 2026

The Shopping List app (package co.kr.ezapp.shoppinglist) does not use accounts and does not store your lists or items on any server. Because of this, there is no "delete my account" step — you are already in full control of your data, on your device.

1. Delete your data yourself, any time

  • All shopping lists and items: open the app → Settings → Reset All Data.
  • Everything, including preferences: Android Settings → Apps → Shopping List → Storage & cache → Clear storage. This resets the app as if newly installed.
  • Everything: uninstalling the app removes all data it stored locally.

None of these require contacting us — the data never leaves your device in the first place.

2. Advertising identifier

The app shows ads through Google AdMob, which mediates ads from several networks — currently including Meta Audience Network — on our behalf. These networks may hold an advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID) tied to your device, not to a Shopping List account. You can reset or delete this identifier independently, at any time, from your device's Settings → Privacy → Ads.

3. Reminder notifications

If you enable the optional daily reminder, Firebase Cloud Messaging assigns your device a messaging token used only to deliver that notification. Turning the reminder off in Settings, or uninstalling the app, invalidates this token.

4. Analytics & crash reports

Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics data (feature usage, crash stack traces) is tied to an anonymous app-install identifier, not your identity, and is not linked to the contents of your shopping lists. If you'd like this associated data deleted, contact us as below with your approximate device model and the date range you used the app — we'll submit a deletion request within 30 days.

5. Contact us

Questions or a formal deletion request: help.ezapp@gmail.com
