Privacy Policy Currency Converter · Effective date: 5 September 2026 This Privacy Policy explains how the Currency Converter app (package com.ezapp.currencyconverter; "we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and shares information about you. 1. Information Collection We do not require you to create an account or provide personal identification information (such as name, email address, or phone number) to use the app. On-device settings: Your currency selections, favourites, and display and notification preferences are stored only locally on your device. They are not transmitted to us. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app permanently removes them. Device information: Our third-party providers below may collect basic device information (such as device model and OS version) and advertising identifiers. 2. Network Requests To look up current and historical exchange rates, the app calls the free, public Frankfurter API (frankfurter.dev). These requests contain only the currency codes and date range being looked up. No personally identifying information is included, and we do not operate servers that store your data. 3. Third-Party Services The app uses the following third-party services, which may collect information used to identify your device: Google AdMob — displays advertisements. Google may collect advertising identifiers (Android Advertising ID) and general usage data to serve, personalise, measure, and limit fraud in ads. See also How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. Firebase Crashlytics — records crash and stability data (crash stack traces, device model, OS version, app state at the time of a crash) so we can diagnose and fix defects. Crash reporting is active only in production releases and is disabled in development builds. 4. Use of Information Information is used to keep the app working and stable, to diagnose crashes, and to show and measure advertisements in compliance with Google's policies. We do not sell your personal information. 5. GDPR (European Users) & CCPA (US Users) GDPR: Users in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland have the right to access, correct, or delete their data. You can manage your consent for personalised ads through the consent dialog shown by Google's advertising SDK, and you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. CCPA: We do not sell your personal information. You have the right to opt out of data collection for advertising purposes via your device's ad settings. 6. Children's Privacy The app is a general-audience utility and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. 7. Data Retention Settings are stored only on your device, so you control retention — clear the app's data or uninstall the app to remove everything it stores locally. Data processed by Google (advertising, crash reporting) is retained and managed according to Google's own policies linked above. 8. Changes to This Policy This policy may be updated from time to time. Material changes will be reflected here with a new effective date. 9.
