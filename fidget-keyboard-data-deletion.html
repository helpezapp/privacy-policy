Data Deletion

Fidget Keyboard  ·  Effective date: 12 September 2026

The Fidget Keyboard app (package com.ezapp.clicker) does not use accounts and does not store your click records, images, or settings on any server. Because of this, there is no "delete my account" step — you are already in full control of your data, on your device.

1. Delete your data yourself, any time

  • Custom images and click records: remove or replace them from within the app's editing tools, or reset a background from Settings.
  • Everything, including preferences: Android Settings → Apps → Fidget Keyboard → Storage & cache → Clear storage. This resets the app as if newly installed.
  • Everything: uninstalling the app removes all data it stored locally, including any cached shared-image files.

None of these require contacting us — the data never leaves your device in the first place.

2. Advertising identifier

The app shows ads through Google AdMob, which mediates ads from several networks — currently including Meta Audience Network — on our behalf. These networks may hold an advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID) tied to your device, not to a Fidget Keyboard account. You can reset or delete this identifier independently, at any time, from your device's Settings → Privacy → Ads.

3. Analytics & crash reports

Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics data (feature usage, crash stack traces) is tied to an anonymous app-install identifier, not your identity, and is not linked to your click records or custom images. If you'd like this associated data deleted, contact us as below with your approximate device model and the date range you used the app — we'll submit a deletion request within 30 days.

4. Contact us

Questions or a formal deletion request: help.ezapp@gmail.com

This page is the data deletion policy referenced by the Fidget Keyboard app's Google Play listing. See also our Privacy Policy.
