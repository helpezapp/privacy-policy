Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Tabata Interval Timer app ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use it.

1. Information We Collect

The App does not require an account and does not collect or transmit personal information to our own servers. All data you create in the App - your custom routines, interval settings, and workout completion history - is stored locally on your device only.

2. Advertising

The App displays ads served by Google AdMob. To serve ads, Google AdMob and its partners may collect and use device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), IP address, and other technical information, and may use this information to show personalized or non-personalized ads. This data collection is governed by Google's own privacy policy, available at https://policies.google.com/privacy. You can control ad personalization in your device's settings (e.g., Settings > Privacy > Ads on Android).

3. Permissions

The App requests internet access solely to load and display ads. It does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, or storage.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties, other than the advertising data described in Section 2, which is collected directly by Google, not by us.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you can delete it at any time by clearing the App's data or uninstalling the App.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

8. Contact Us
