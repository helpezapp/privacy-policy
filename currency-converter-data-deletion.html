Data Deletion Request

Currency Converter  ·  App package: com.ezapp.currencyconverter  ·  Last updated: 5 September 2026

The Currency Converter app does not use accounts and does not store your data on our servers. Almost all data stays on your own device, so you can delete it yourself at any time. This page explains how.

1. Delete all data stored by the app on your device

Your currency selections, favourites, and display and notification preferences are stored only on your device. To remove them, do either of the following:

  • In the app: open Settings → Reset All Data and confirm. This clears every preference the app has saved.
  • Uninstall the app, or go to your device's Settings → Apps → Currency Converter → Storage → Clear data.

2. Advertising identifier

Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which may use your device's advertising ID. You can delete or reset this identifier at any time in your device settings: Settings → Privacy → Ads → Delete advertising ID (or Reset advertising ID).

3. Crash and diagnostics data

Production releases send crash reports to Firebase Crashlytics (a Google service) so we can fix defects. This data (crash stack traces, device model, OS version) is not linked to your identity. To request deletion of crash data associated with your device, contact us at the address below and include your device model and the approximate dates of use.

4. Contact for deletion requests

For any request to delete data related to your use of the app, email help.ezapp@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

This page is referenced by the Currency Converter app's Google Play Data safety section. See also the Privacy Policy.
