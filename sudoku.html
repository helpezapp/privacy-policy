Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Sudoku app ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use it.

1. Information We Collect

The App does not require an account and does not collect or transmit personal information to our own servers. All data you create in the App - your games in progress, statistics, daily puzzle records, hint usage, and settings - is stored locally on your device only.

The App uses the third-party services described below (Google AdMob and Firebase Crashlytics), which collect limited device and usage information directly.

2. Advertising

The App displays banner ads and optional rewarded ads served by Google AdMob. Rewarded ads are shown only when you choose to watch one, for example to get an extra hint, to continue a game after too many mistakes, or to hide banner ads for a limited time. To serve ads, Google AdMob and its partners may collect and use device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), IP address, and other technical information, and may use this information to show personalized or non-personalized ads. This data collection is governed by Google's own privacy policy, available at https://policies.google.com/privacy. You can control ad personalization in your device's settings (e.g., Settings > Privacy > Ads on Android).

If you are in a region where consent is required (such as the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland), the App asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before any ads are requested. You can review or change your choice at any time in the App under Settings > Privacy options.

3. Crash Reports

The App uses Firebase Crashlytics, a service provided by Google, to receive reports when the App crashes so we can fix problems. A crash report contains technical information such as the device model, operating system version, app version, the state of the App at the time of the crash, and a randomly generated installation identifier. Crash reports do not include your name, email address, or the content of your games, and they are used only to diagnose and fix problems. Firebase's data handling is described at https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

4. Permissions

The App requests internet access solely to load and display ads and to send crash reports. If you turn on the daily puzzle reminder, the App asks for permission to show notifications and re-registers the reminder after the device restarts. It does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, or storage.

5. Reviews

The App may invite you to rate it using Google Play's in-app review feature. Any rating or review you submit is handled by Google Play under Google's privacy policy; the App never sees what you write.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share it with third parties, other than the advertising data described in Section 2 and the crash reports described in Section 3, which are collected directly by Google (AdMob and Firebase), not by us.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you can delete it at any time with Settings > Reset all data in the App, by clearing the App's data, or by uninstalling the App. See also our Data Deletion page.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
