Privacy Policy

Fidget Keyboard  ·  Effective date: 12 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Fidget Keyboard app (package com.ezapp.clicker; "we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and shares information about you.

1. Information Collection

We do not require you to create an account or provide personal identification information (such as name, email address, or phone number) to use the app.

  • On-device data: Your click records, custom background images, and settings are stored only locally on your device. They are not uploaded to our servers. Clearing the app's data, or uninstalling the app, permanently removes them.
  • Daily reminder notifications are scheduled entirely on your device (no server involved) and can be turned off in Settings at any time.
  • Device information: Our third-party providers below may collect basic device information (such as device model and OS version) and advertising identifiers.

2. Third-Party Services

The app uses the following third-party services, which may collect information used to identify your device:

  • Google AdMob — displays advertisements, mediating ads from several ad networks — currently including Meta Audience Network — on our behalf. Each network may independently collect advertising identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID) to serve and measure ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, from your device's Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Meta Audience Network — an AdMob mediation partner that may independently collect advertising identifiers and app-usage data to serve and measure ads on our behalf.
  • Firebase Analytics — used to understand feature usage, tied to an anonymous app-install identifier, not your identity.
  • Firebase Crashlytics — records crash and stability data so we can diagnose and fix defects.

3. User Content and Sharing

Images you choose for customisation are stored only on your device — we do not upload them to any server. When you share your screen from the app, the generated image is stored temporarily in your device's cache and shared through the sharing method you pick. Responsibility for shared content lies entirely with you.

4. Use of Information

Information is used to keep the app working and stable, to diagnose crashes, to understand which features are used, and to show and measure advertisements in compliance with Google's and Meta's policies. We do not sell your personal information.

5. GDPR (European Users) & CCPA (US Users)

  • GDPR: Users in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland have the right to access, correct, or delete their data. You can manage your consent for personalised ads through the consent dialog shown by Google's advertising SDK, and reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.
  • CCPA: We do not sell your personal information. You have the right to opt out of data collection for advertising purposes via your device's ad settings.

6. Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Material changes will be reflected here with a new effective date.

8. Contact Us

Questions about this Privacy Policy: help.ezapp@gmail.com
